# Narration language and duration

For all routes, explicit user language/dialect choices prevail. For a new project without such a choice, preserve the route/style default (English for A1–A5 and B common fallback), and state the resolved `narration_language` in the proposal/project brief. Reuse approved settings. Conversation language controls guidance, not automatic video translation. Subtitle/publishing languages may differ when requested. Do not add a mandatory language question when a known choice or default suffices.

## A1–A5

Write exact spoken lines in `narration_language`; keep model instructions and frozen voice-direction blocks in English. Include the spoken language and compatible accent in that frozen profile. Do not apply a General American English sample profile to Vietnamese speech unchanged. Resolve a requested Vietnamese regional accent when supplied; otherwise propose a suitable Vietnamese profile in Phase A without a separate approval gate. State it as a proposed direction, not a provider-tested guarantee.

Translate for reference only when the planning language differs or the user asks. Do not translate approved speech just to satisfy an English prompt template. Optional post-production overlays use the chosen publishing language.

## Timing

For English drafting only, roughly 20–25 words per ten-second clip can be a starting estimate. Do not reuse that word-count target for Vietnamese or other languages: whitespace-separated units and speaking rhythms differ. Estimate by reading the exact line at the proposed natural pace, allowing breaths, pauses and action beats; label unmeasured duration as an estimate. When recording/generation is authorized and available, measure the actual audio and inspect/listen before certifying fit. Never claim a read-aloud or listening check that was not performed.

Shorten or redistribute copy before speeding speech up. Keep natural sentence boundaries, meaning and the approved delivery. For A1–A5 fit the spoken segment inside its approximately ten-second clip. B follows the actual original-speed narration and its existing alignment rules. Language changes to accepted speech require the existing scoped revision/approval process; do not automatically regenerate or revoice accepted media. Check Vietnamese diacritics and glyph coverage in captions and thumbnails using the actual output.
