# pop

Cue it on the frame the element lands, not when it starts moving. Several pops in a row step down in volume and alternate with `tick` (promo sound S2).

Cue it from a word of the narration (`frameAtWord`) or a promo beat (`frameAtSceneBeat`), never a typed frame.
