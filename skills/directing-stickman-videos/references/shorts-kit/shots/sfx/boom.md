# boom

Place it after the strong word, never on it: the word needs its beat of silence first. On a death toll or a disaster, if used at all, quiet and after the number.

Cue it from a word of the narration (`frameAtWord`) or a promo beat (`frameAtSceneBeat`), never a typed frame.
