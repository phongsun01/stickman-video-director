# whoosh

Put it on the move, not after it: the sound starts as the camera starts. One whoosh per real jump; a film that whooshes on every cut has told the ear nothing.

Cue it from a word of the narration (`frameAtWord`) or a promo beat (`frameAtSceneBeat`), never a typed frame.
