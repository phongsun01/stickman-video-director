# tick

One tick per counted item, so the ear counts with the eye. When the count accelerates, the ticks accelerate with it; past about 8 per second, let them thin out.

Cue it from a word of the narration (`frameAtWord`) or a promo beat (`frameAtSceneBeat`), never a typed frame.
