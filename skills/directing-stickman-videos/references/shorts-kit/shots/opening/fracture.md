name: fracture
summary: A 5×5 grid of tiles assembles from a 3D shattered state into a full poster, ring by ring in a ripple from the center. The text lights up for one beat, then all the pieces fly off screen, spinning faster as they move away from the center
use: The opening first shot, "from chaos to form", for a brand or poster reveal; reversed, or using only the second half, it works as a hard transition
length: about 5.2s (156f@30fps; assemble 0–2.6s · hold 1s · scatter 1.5s)
energy: high (high at both ends, still in the middle; suits a BGM accent at the start and end)

## Intent
Film "the product taking shape" as a physical event: pieces fly back to their places from 3D space, each spinning, and the viewer watches a complete picture grow out of disorder. After the hold for reading the text, everything blasts out of frame and hands attention cleanly to the next shot. Entry and exit are symmetric, so one asset can be used both ways.

## Motion core
- Each of the 25 tiles holds its own deterministic pseudo-random start pose: offset ±450/±300/±490px (x/y/z) + ±180° rotation on all three axes, generated by `rand(seed)` so renders are reproducible
- The entrance staggers as a ripple by Manhattan distance: `delay = (|r-2|+|c-2|) * 0.045`. The center settles first and the four corners last; each tile takes 0.34 of duration on `bezier(0.42,0,0.3,1)` (a long deceleration before landing). About 10f after landing there is one damped rotateX wobble of ≤2.5°, the "click" of seating into the slot
- The 25 tiles cut **the same poster** (each tile takes its own cell by grid-position offset), and the full picture only shows once they are put together; tiles are shaded by orientation (the more edge-on to the camera, the darker)
- Opacity rises to 1 quickly with `tin * 2.5`: the pieces are nearly solid even from far away, which avoids a "fade-in feel" diluting the physical feel
- The poster is printed with the eyebrow INTRODUCING + the shorts-kit wordmark (logo + lowercase wordmark), sliced along with the tiles. It lights up at t=0.42–0.52 and holds the eye during the hold; the exit has no separate fade, and the text and logo are torn away along with the pieces. During the hold the whole poster pushes in very slowly by 3%
- The exit does not fade: each tile flies 620–880px out along the direction away from the screen center (computed with `atan2` + ±0.8 jitter) while adding ±150° of spin, accelerating with `inCubic`, so the solid pieces leave the frame

## Parameters
| Parameter | Typical value | Tuning feel |
|------|--------|----------|
| Grid | 5×5, 380×230px, perspective 900px | More tiles make a finer ripple but each tile has less presence; perspective <700 exaggerates the perspective and breaks easily |
| Initial scatter | dx±450 / dy±300 / dz −210~+490 | A larger scatter makes the opening more chaotic, but the assembly path gets longer and the delay window must grow with it |
| Ripple interval | 0.045 per ring (Manhattan distance) | >0.07 and the ripple turns into "roll call, ring by ring"; <0.03 and everything lands almost at once, losing the ripple |
| Entrance duration | 0.34 per tile (inOutCubic) | Shorter makes the shape come together with a snap; together with the ripple interval, make sure the last tile lands by ≤0.6 |
| Exit window | takeoff at 0.70 + delay×0.5, duration 0.18 (inCubic) | **The window must end before t≈0.97**: with 0.72+delay×0.7/0.26 the corner tiles once failed to leave the frame and got stuck at the frame edge for a second (precedent 2026-08-05) |
| Exit distance | 620–880px (away from center + random jitter) | Too short a distance leaves pieces at the frame edge; the center tile's direction is random (there is no "away from center" for it) |
| Poster color | `ACCENT_HUE=218` derives the whole poster (cool dark field + soft top-left light + glowing horizon arc at the bottom) | Change one hue constant to recolor the whole thing; seams are 0.5 design px, and wider ones cut strokes out of the text |

## Known traps
- The exit leaves by displacement, not by opacity fade. A fade reads as "vanish", not "fly away", and the physical feel is lost. When changing parameters, keep `opacity = min(1, tin*2.5)` with no exit term attached
- All random amounts must go through the deterministic `rand(seed)` (equivalent to Remotion `random()`); true randomness jitters between renders
- The hold (t≈0.6–0.7) is the only window for reading the text. If you lengthen the copy, widen the gap between the label fade-out and the first tile's takeoff too, or the tiles scatter before the text is read
- Keep the logo from crossing a seam: a seam cuts a thin gap or ragged edge through the logo and reads as a broken image. The demo centers the wordmark on the center of row 3's tile and sets the font size to 22, so the logo falls entirely inside the column 2 tile, and when the pieces scatter the logo flies off whole with one tile. Recompute these when changing the logo size or the grid
- When used as a transition, take only the scatter section at t=0.7–1, played reversed or forward for each half. Footage on the two sides, each pasted on the tile faces, needs separate texture mapping
